From variability to radius

Black hole images derived directly from the X-ray power spectrum under the simplified assumption that frequencies correspond to viscous timescales, which map to radii as:

r(ν) = rg [α (H/R)² / (ν tg)]2/3

tg = GM/c³

The dotted line marks 2 RG.

Stochastic azimuthal structure has been added for aesthetic reasons, and (unlike the radii of emission) is not inferred from the data.

Rotation has similarly been added but is purely illustrative and set at 0.01% of the local Keplerian rate.

Sound and interpretation

The spectrum above a low-frequency cutoff of 0.3 Hz is shifted upward by a fixed factor of 64: a 4–8 Hz QPO becomes 256–512 Hz. The first four bins (0.0625–0.25 Hz) are muted to suppress misleading low-frequency pulsation from treating these finite bands as pure tones. Tone amplitude is proportional to √max(P − 2, 0), using one shared gain. The QPO is not given any extra emphasis. Sound holds the last measured spectrum during gaps; these holds are not new measurements. Playback and seeking resume sound only while the movie is playing.

Disc brightness uses fixed logarithmic scaling, frequency smoothing, and a faint data-present glow. Blue rings illustrate the inferred launch times; their size and speed do not measure jet propagation. Ejection dates carry published uncertainties, shown as shaded bands.
